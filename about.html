About Me

Data Scientist & Data Analyst turning raw data into decisions.

Power BI, SQL, Python, and machine learning - from cleaning messy datasets to shipping dashboards people actually use.

I'm comfortable working independently in fast-paced, ambiguous environments with strict adherence to process and quality benchmarks. My work spans BI reporting (Power BI, SQL, Excel) and ML-driven data classification, and I enjoy the moment where a messy dataset finally becomes a clear, trustworthy story.
